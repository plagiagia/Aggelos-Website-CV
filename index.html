About the artist

Aggelos Giannoulis

A visual artist working with colored pencil, drawing and painting in Thessaloniki, Greece.

His work explores intimacy, identity and vulnerability through figurative and abstract imagery. Colored pencil on paper is central to his practice, alongside oil, acrylic, mixed media and printmaking.

He graduated from the School of Fine and Applied Arts at Aristotle University of Thessaloniki in 2025, with an Integrated Master’s degree in Painting.
