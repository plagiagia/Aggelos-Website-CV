Artist bio

Aggelos Giannoulis is a contemporary visual artist working primarily with colored pencil on paper, alongside oil, acrylic, mixed media, and printmaking. He holds a distinction-level Integrated Master’s degree in Painting from Aristotle University of Thessaloniki (School of Fine & Applied Arts).

His practice explores intimacy, vulnerability, embodiment, and pop-cultural fragments—building images where the personal and the social collide.

Thesis series (June 2025)

“Η Σωτηρία του κόσμου ήταν έναν οργασμό μακριά” is a research-led studio project presented as a cohesive body of 20+ works. The series investigates moments of intensity and presence, asking what “salvation” could mean when experienced as surrender, connection, and radical acceptance.
