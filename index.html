About

Biography

Aggelos Giannoulis (b. Greece) is a contemporary visual artist working primarily with colored pencil on paper, alongside oil, acrylic, mixed media, and printmaking. He holds a distinction-level Integrated Master’s degree in Painting from the Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, School of Fine & Applied Arts (2019–2025, final grade 9.37/10; thesis 9.85/10).

His practice explores intimacy, vulnerability, embodiment, and the residue of popular culture — building images where the personal and the social collide. The thesis series “Η Σωτηρία του κόσμου ήταν έναν οργασμό μακριά” (June 2025) presents twenty-plus works circling moments of intensity and presence, asking what salvation might mean when felt as surrender, connection, and radical acceptance.

Lives and works in Thessaloniki, Greece. Available for exhibitions, commissions, and collaborations.

Artist bio

Aggelos Giannoulis is a contemporary visual artist working primarily with colored pencil on paper, alongside oil, acrylic, mixed media, and printmaking. He holds a distinction-level Integrated Master’s degree in Painting from Aristotle University of Thessaloniki (School of Fine & Applied Arts).

His practice explores intimacy, vulnerability, embodiment, and pop-cultural fragments—building images where the personal and the social collide.

Thesis series (June 2025)

“Η Σωτηρία του κόσμου ήταν έναν οργασμό μακριά” is a research-led studio project presented as a cohesive body of 20+ works. The series investigates moments of intensity and presence, asking what “salvation” could mean when experienced as surrender, connection, and radical acceptance.
