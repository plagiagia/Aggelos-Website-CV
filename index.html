Studio note

The work moves slowly through touch: paper, pressure, layers of color, and the uneasy charge of images already circulating around us.

Giannoulis builds scenes where vulnerability, embodiment, desire, and social residue collide. The drawings keep their craft visible, letting small marks carry the weight of confession, performance, and recognition.

About

Biography and practice

Aggelos Giannoulis is a contemporary visual artist working primarily with colored pencil on paper, alongside oil, acrylic, mixed media, and printmaking.

He holds a distinction-level Integrated Master of Fine Arts in Painting from Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, School of Fine and Applied Arts. His thesis series presents twenty-plus works circling moments of intensity, surrender, connection, and radical acceptance.
