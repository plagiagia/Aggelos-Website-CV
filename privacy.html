Privacy

When you send an enquiry, Aggelos Giannoulis receives your name, email address, subject and message so he can respond. Please do not include sensitive personal information.

Where your enquiry goes

Enquiries are stored in a private database provided by Supabase and accessed through the artist’s password-protected studio. Vercel hosts the website and processes requests. Messages are not displayed publicly or used for marketing.

Your choices

To ask about your information or request deletion, email aggelos.giannoul@gmail.com. You can also contact this address directly instead of using the form.

Cookies and abuse prevention

The public gallery does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Signing in to the artist studio sets an essential session cookie that expires after eight hours. A hashed network identifier is used to limit repeated form submissions; expired rate-limit records are removed during subsequent requests.
